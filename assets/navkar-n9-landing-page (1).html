FAQs

Before you book.

Is Navkar suitable for families?
Yes. Navkar is designed as a welcoming, safe and festive experience for families, friends and dance lovers of all ages.
Do I need to know Garba or Dandiya?
Not at all. Whether you are an experienced dancer or joining the circle for the first time, you are welcome at Navkar. The 3 October workshop is there if you want a head start.
Tell me about the workshop.
A two-hour Garba workshop on 3 October 2026, one day only, ₹300 per person. Beginner-friendly, and you can collect your festival pass at the Pass Lounge on the same day.
Who is performing?
Bamboo Beats from Mumbai are our live band for all nine nights, and Tarun Khanna joins us on Wednesday, 14 October.
Where is the event happening?
Navkar Navratri Utsav Season 9 takes place at Jalvihar, Necklace Road, Hyderabad.
What are the dates and timings?
The festival runs 11–19 October 2026. Gates open at Confirm 6:30 / 7:00 PM.
What do the passes cost?
₹499 a single night, ₹2,000 for the Season Pass covering all nine nights, ₹1,800 for a Group of 4, ₹4,800 for a Group of 10, ₹299 for children under four and ₹300 for the workshop.
Is the venue vegetarian and alcohol-free?
Yes. Navkar maintains a strictly pure-vegetarian and alcohol-free environment, and Jain food is available.
What should I wear?
Traditional festive wear is encouraged and the nightly theme competitions are judged on it, but it is not enforced at the gate.
What happens if it rains?
The celebration continues. The venue has both a covered arena and an air-conditioned arena.
Final call
